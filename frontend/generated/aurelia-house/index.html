01 — Intent

A quiet building
on a loud city.

The concept asks a single question: what would a home in Gurugram feel like if it were designed for quiet first?

AURELIA House is a fictional residential concept developed by Aurelia Living as a design exercise. It is set in Gurugram, Haryana, and is not a real, registered, approved or purchasable development. No site has been acquired, no plans have been sanctioned, and no approvals exist.

The design intent is metropolitan calm. A low, horizontal arrival sequence; a deep-set entrance loggia; a double-height living volume that opens toward a planted court. Materials are limited to three families — honed stone, low-iron glass, and oiled timber — so that the building reads as a composition of planes rather than a stack of parts.

Where a brief would normally carry marketing language, this concept carries restraint. Anything that could not be verified — unit sizes, pricing, possession, amenity schedules, distances, approvals — is deliberately left blank and marked Details on request rather than filled in.

Visuals are illustrative architectural studies generated for this demonstration. They do not depict an existing building and must not be read as a record of one.

02 — Design chapters

Seven moves,
one idea.

Each chapter below is a design position, not a product claim. Where a real project would publish a schedule of finishes or a clubhouse inventory, this concept publishes only intent.

Chapter 01

Arrival

A three-step descent from street to forecourt, screened by a stone wall and a shallow water channel. The door is placed so the city is behind you, not in front of you.

Study · Street edge

Chapter 02

Architecture

Horizontal bands rather than vertical towers. Deep reveals, expressed slab edges, and a limited material palette so the mass reads calm at street level and light from above.

Study · Massing logic

Chapter 03

Interiors

One continuous timber-lined volume per home. Services are consolidated into a spine so the living space stays uninterrupted, and daylight is drawn deep into the plan.

Study · Interior section

Chapter 04

Landscape

A planted court at the centre of the plan, with a shaded walking loop and a single water body. Landscape here is treated as architecture — graded, framed, and kept low against the sight lines.

Position · No programme published

Chapter 05

Clubhouse

The concept reserves a shared pavilion at the arrival edge. Its size, contents and operation are deliberately undefined — an amenity schedule would imply a real development, which this is not.

Position · Details on request

Chapter 06

Wellness

Quiet first: shaded thresholds, cross-ventilation, and rooms that hold low light. Wellness is treated as an architectural condition rather than a list of equipment.

Position · Not a facility claim

Chapter 07

Lifestyle

The intended life is small-scale and unhurried: arrive, cross a shaded court, enter a quiet volume. The concept assumes a household of one or two, working from home part of the week, and treating privacy as the primary amenity.

Because no unit programme exists, no lifestyle claim about occupancy, community size or facilities is made here.

Position · Illustrative only

"The most metropolitan gesture a building can make
is to be quiet on the street."

The palette follows that instruction. Navy carries depth, pearl carries calm, and copper appears only where a hand would touch something — a reveal, a rail, an edge.

06 — Questions

Straight answers,
including the
uncomfortable ones.

The most important question on a concept page is whether the pictures are real. These answers are written to make that unambiguous.

Is AURELIA House a real property?
No. AURELIA House is a fictional design concept produced by Aurelia Living to demonstrate architectural direction. It is not a real, registered, sanctioned or purchasable development. No land has been acquired and no approvals exist.
Are the images photographs of a building?
No. Every image on this page is an illustrative vector study built to communicate massing, light and material intent. None is a photograph, none shows an existing structure, and none is documentary. They should never be read as evidence that a building was designed, approved or constructed.
What is the price?
On Request. No price is published because none exists. Publishing a figure for a fictional project would present a fabrication as a commercial term.
What configurations and sizes are available?
Details on request. The concept does not define a unit mix, saleable areas, carpet areas or room dimensions. The plan and section images in the gallery are schematic diagrams explicitly marked as not to scale.
Is the project RERA registered?
Not applicable. No RERA registration exists or is claimed for this concept, and no registration number appears on this page. Any registration number would be invented, and inventing one is unlawful. For reference, genuine registrations can only be verified on the official state RERA portals.
Where exactly is it located?
At city level only: Gurugram, Haryana, India. No pin, coordinate, parcel, sector or road is published, and no map tile is embedded. The diagram in the location section is an abstract urban-context graphic, not a site map.
How far is it from the airport, offices or schools?
No distance or commute time is stated anywhere on this page, and no landmark is named. Quoting verified travel times for a location that does not exist would be an invention presented as a fact.
What amenities are provided?
None are claimed. The design chapters describe intent for landscape, clubhouse, wellness and lifestyle — positions, not a facility schedule. An amenity list would imply an operating development.
When is possession?
Details on request. There is no construction programme, no launch phase and no completion date behind this concept.
How do I contact the sales team or book a site visit?
There is no sales team. No phone number, email address, sales office or site-visit booking exists for a fictional project, so none is given here — inventing contact details would be misleading. The enquiry form below is a demonstration form that does not transmit or store anything.
Who is behind the concept?
Aurelia Living, named as the fictional concept author. It is not a registered promoter, developer or company, and no corporate, track-record or heritage claims are made anywhere on this page.
Is this an authorised channel partner listing?
No. There is no broker, channel partner, agency or brokerage arrangement involved. No listing partnership disclosure appears on this page because none exists.
What should I take away from this page?
The design direction: a Contemporary Urban Sanctuary language of horizontal massing, three material families, and deep-set thresholds. Use it as a reference for architectural intent. Do not use it as a basis for any property decision.
