01 — Project Overview

Built for a higher
way of living.

Prestige Parklane is a residential address in Gurugram planned across 11.76 acres, where the landscape is treated as the primary shared space and the buildings are arranged around it.

Prestige Parklane is developed by Prestige Group, with the project promoted by Apex Realty Management Pvt Ltd, a Prestige Group company. Nine towers of 3B + G + 24 floors hold 1,788 apartments across three configurations: 1 BHK Studio & Suite, 2 BHK Premium Classic and 3 BHK Grand Horizon.

Seventy-five per cent of the site is held as open space. What fills it is deliberate rather than leftover: a main pool and a kids pool, a gathering arena, a party lawn, tower courtyards, a jogging track, a viewing deck and a pet garden, distributed across the parcel so daily use happens through the whole campus.

Inside, a clubhouse of roughly 34,500 square feet carries the indoor side of community life, with indoor sports, a gymnasium and a community hall alongside the utilities a residential campus runs on. Construction is contracted to L&T Construction, and project escrow accounts are held at ICICI Bank.

02 — Architecture

Architecture
with intent.

The plan is organised in three moves: hold the ground as landscape, keep the towers apart on a shared podium, and put water and courts where the site is used most.

01 — The arrangement

Nine towers on one shared ground.

Nine residential towers are planned on an 11.76-acre parcel, each at 3B + G + 24 floors. A shared podium keeps three basement levels and the ground floor off the landscape, so the towers stand apart and read as separate markers rather than as a single wall.

02 — The open ground

Seventy-five per cent stays open.

Seventy-five per cent of the site is held as open space, and it is programmed rather than left over: pools, lawns, courtyards, a jogging track, a viewing deck and a pet garden, spread across the campus so that the landscape is what residents move through each day.

03 — The Signature Experience

Five places the
day actually happens.

Taken from the project record: the spaces that carry the club, the water, the landscape and the evening.

01

The Grand Clubhouse

A ~34,500 sq ft private social clubhouse with a temperature-controlled indoor pool and lounge, the indoor anchor of a campus where three quarters of the site is left open.

02

The water

A resort-grade infinity swimming pool flanked by palm trees, sun decks and submerged loungers, planned alongside a separate kids pool inside the landscape.

03

The landscaped grounds

Over 75% curated open biophilic space, with walking paths, reflexology trails and meditation nooks laid through the middle of the campus rather than along its edge.

04

The active ground

Floodlit tennis and squash courts, multi-sport turf, padel and pickleball courts, a multi-court, a cricket practice net, an outdoor gym and dedicated jogging circuits.

05

The evening

Illuminated water bodies, fountain plazas and private party decks, with a kids adventure play grove set apart from the evening water so the two do not compete.

05 — The Residences

Every detail,
considered.

Three configurations, from a 323 sq.ft studio to a 1,240 sq.ft three-bedroom home. Layout drawings and finish schedules are released with the brochure and on request; they are not published here.

1 BHK Studio & Suite runs 323 – 485 sq.ft and opens at ₹65 Lakh*. 2 BHK Premium Classic runs 720 – 795 sq.ft at ₹1.15 Cr*. 3 BHK Grand Horizon runs 1,120 – 1,240 sq.ft at ₹1.65 Cr*. All three sit inside the same nine-tower campus, so the open ground, the clubhouse and the pool deck are shared across the range rather than tiered by configuration.
