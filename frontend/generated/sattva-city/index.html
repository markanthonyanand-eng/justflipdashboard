Inside The Home

Specifications, as per the developer's brochure

Structure & Flooring

RCC structure. Vitrified tiles in the foyer, living, dining, corridors and all bedrooms; ceramic tiles in balconies; vitrified tiles in the kitchen and utility.

Doors & Windows

Main door in a timber frame with a polished flush shutter; internal doors in timber frames with laminated flush shutters. UPVC or aluminium framed external doors and windows with clear glass and provision for mosquito mesh.

Toilets

Vitrified tiles for flooring and walls up to the false ceiling, countertop wash basins, EWCs with chrome-plated fittings, shower mixers and geysers in all toilets. The top two floors of each tower get solar water heating along with geysers.

Kitchen

Vitrified tile flooring in the kitchen and utility, with provision for an exhaust fan. Counters and dado are left for the owner's own kitchen design.

Electrical

Concealed PVC-insulated copper wiring with modular switches, TV and data points in the living room and all bedrooms, and a telephone point in the living room.

Lobbies & Lifts

Elegant ground floor lobby flooring, vitrified tiles in the basement and upper floor lobbies, lift cladding in marble, granite or quartz as per the architect's design, and lifts of suitable size and capacity on all floors.
